How it works

Six steps from one plug's reading to advice you can act on.

Click the plug to see what is inside

Power passes straight through; the plug only measures. These are the usual parts of an energy-monitoring plug, drawn as a sketch, not a wiring diagram. A real one would be a certified plug, not something built from mains parts.

CONCEPTThe plug sits between the wall socket and the appliance's own plug. The hardware is not built yet.

1 · SMART PLUG, HTTPMeasureA plug on each appliance posts its watts every minute.
2 · STATISTICSLearnThe app builds each appliance's own normal, hour by hour.
3 · STATISTICS + AIDetectStatistics check each appliance. A small neural network adds a second layer for what those checks miss.
4 · NAMED CHECKSNameThe alert says what kind of problem it is.
5 · BAND TARIFFPriceIt becomes ringgit a month on your bill.
6 · RULES + AIAdviseIt says what to do, or plans a cut to hit the bill you want. A language model writes the chat answer from those findings.

How it detects

Two layers, both learned from each appliance's own readings. A new plug is not judged until it has 14 days of history.

LAYER 1 · NAMED CHECKS
  • Statistics on what is normal for this appliance: running watts, usual hours, share of time on
  • Every 5 minutes it looks for: plug not reporting, on at an unusual time, drawing more power than usual, running more of the time
  • Says what kind of problem it is
LAYER 2 · SMALL NEURAL NETWORK
  • An autoencoder (25 → 5 → 25), written by hand, one per appliance
  • Learns the shape of a normal 24 hours, then flags a day it cannot reconstruct on three hourly checks in a row
  • Catches patterns the named checks did not anticipate

A finding must persist before it becomes an alert, and each problem gets one alert. On real recordings with 120 injected faults: a fixed watt limit caught 7, layer 1 alone 57, both layers 66.

Real, simulated, assumed

What this demo is and is not.

REAL
  • Appliance power recordings the house replays (AMPds2, one house in Canada)
  • Sarawak Energy's domestic tariff and its band rule
SIMULATED
  • The house, its plugs and its fast clock
  • Every fault: we inject it, the app has to find it
ASSUMED
  • The water heater's wattage and schedule
AI USE
  • A small neural network per appliance learns its normal day and flags an unusual one
  • A language model writes the Advisor's chat answers from the computed findings; it cannot add figures
  • Not AI: the bill, the tariff and the advice are arithmetic and rules
NOT BUILT
  • Real smart plug hardware
  • Switching appliances off remotely
